# Buffalo Wing Magnet — No-AMS 2.5D Press-Fit Fridge Magnet

Represent the 716 on your fridge, toolbox, or tailgate cooler!

This Buffalo Wing Magnet is a 3-part, 2.5D layered fridge magnet inspired by Buffalo's favorite game-day snack and iconic stripe silhouette. Modeled from scratch in OpenSCAD, it is engineered for crisp multi-color printing on any single-extruder 3D printer—no AMS, MMU, or mid-print filament swaps required.

The orange wing body and green celery streak sit 1.3 mm proud of the white backing tray for bold, tactile depth, with 0.10 mm friction-fit pockets and 0.20 mm inset glue relief wells on the pocket floors.

MODEL VARIANTS & PROJECT FILES

• Plate 1 (Base_White): 4.8 mm white backing tray with 2.0 mm recessed pockets, 0.20 mm glue relief wells, and dual 5x2 mm rear magnet pockets (to prevent spinning).
• Plate 2 (Wing_Orange): 3.3 mm scalloped orange wing insert (sits 1.3 mm proud).
• Plate 3 (Celery_Green): 3.3 mm tapered green celery stick insert (sits 1.3 mm proud).
• OpenSCAD Source: Includes a single_10x2 magnet toggle if you prefer one 10x2 mm centered magnet instead of dual 5x2 mm magnets.

RECOMMENDED PRINT SETTINGS

• Orientation: Flat on the build plate, face up (no supports needed).
• Material: PLA or PLA+ (stiff filament gives the cleanest press-fit).
• Layer Height: 0.20 mm (or 0.16 mm for finer top-surface relief).
• Wall Generator: Arachne (mandatory to cleanly resolve the narrow celery tip and tight wing notches).
• Wall Loops: 3 perimeters.
• Infill: 15% Gyroid.
• Elephant Foot Compensation: 0.15 mm (helps inserts seat cleanly without trimming).

HARDWARE & ASSEMBLY

• Magnets: 2x 5x2 mm neodymium disc magnets (default STL) or 1x 10x2 mm magnet (via OpenSCAD).
• Optional CA Glue: Tiny drop in the 0.20 mm floor relief wells and magnet pockets for permanent retention.
• Assembly: Let parts cool completely on the build plate, dry-fit the wing and celery inserts into the white base tray, press firmly until seated, and press the magnet(s) into the rear pockets.

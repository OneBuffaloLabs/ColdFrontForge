Contest entries, multi-material models, and print profiles designed for MakerWorld competitions.

## Active & Past Contests

- [Halloween 2026 Design Contest](./halloween-2026/CONTEST.md) — Spooky, functional, and decorative Halloween designs.
  - **Entry 1:** [Billy the Puppet Sphere](./halloween-2026/entry-1-billy-the-puppet/) — Friction-fit multi-part display sphere.

## Guidelines

- Ensure proper plate color assignments for multi-color toolchains.
- Export native 3MF files containing verified print profiles, brim choices, and support boundaries.
- Include explicit dimensional tolerances for moving parts and assembly snaps.

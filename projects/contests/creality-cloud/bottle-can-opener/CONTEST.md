## Contest Overview & Criteria

- **Theme Focus:** Functional tools designed to open bottles (crown caps or twist-offs), pop soda/can pull-tabs, or provide ergonomic mechanical leverage.
- **Key Design Requirements:**
  - Must be 100% original work or an explicitly attributed valid open-source remix.
  - Functional mechanical durability: Part must withstand repeated leverage and torque forces without layer shearing.
  - Printability: Optimized for FDM printing without unnecessary supports; clean print-in-place or single-piece profile.
  - Multi-functionality is favored (e.g., dual-purpose cap lifter + can tab pry).

## Entry 1 Notes: Frost Bite

- **Concept:** Compact, winter/snowflake-themed keychain tool.
- **Primary Functions:**
  - Crown cap pry tooth reinforced against horizontal shear forces.
  - Slanted wedge arm for slipping under soda/beverage can tabs.
  - Integrated 4.0 mm through-hole for split key rings.
- **Print Strategy:** Flat bed orientation with solid 4–5 perimeters to ensure high torque resistance across the lever arms.

## Contest Overview & Criteria

- **Theme Focus:** Functional tools designed to open bottles (crown caps or twist-offs), pop soda/can pull-tabs, or provide ergonomic mechanical leverage.
- **Key Design Requirements:**
  - Must be 100% original work or an explicitly attributed valid open-source remix.
  - Functional mechanical durability: Part must withstand repeated leverage and torque forces without layer shearing.
  - Printability: Optimized for FDM printing without unnecessary supports; clean print-in-place or single-piece profile.
  - Multi-functionality and ergonomics favored.

## Entry 1 Notes: Frost Bite

- **Concept:** Compact, winter/snowflake-themed keychain tool designed for leverage-assisted beverage tab opening.
- **Primary Functions:**
  - 24 mm internal pocket that slides over beverage pull tabs to eliminate fingernail strain.
  - Slim 5.0 mm flat body profile for EDC pocket carry.
  - Integrated 4.2 mm through-hole for standard split keyrings.
- **Print Strategy:** Support-free flat bed orientation with 4 perimeters and 25%–30% Gyroid infill for maximum layer-to-layer prying strength. Multi-color variants configured with prime tower "No sparse layers (beta)" optimization.

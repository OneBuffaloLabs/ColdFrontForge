# K2 SE Tool Kit Holder — Side Enclosure Mount

I ran into a storage issue with my tools scattering around my desk and noticed there were no dedicated side-mount organizers available for the K2 SE yet. I designed this holder to mount directly to the side panels of the machine so every maintenance tool stays organized, upright, and out of the way.

The backplate lines up with the stock mounting holes on the transparent enclosure panels. It includes dedicated, snug slots for the entire stock tool kit, including flush cutters, scraper, grease, glue stick, USB drive, wrench, Allen keys, and an accessory hook for hanging calipers or brushes.

FEATURES

• Dedicated Tool Slots: Custom-fit cavities for your scraper, flush cutters, glue stick (25 mm), grease tube (19 mm), USB drive, wrench, and full set of Allen keys.
• Enclosure Mount: Anchors directly to the transparent PC panels of the official K2 SE / K1 SE enclosure using stock screw spacing (104.96 mm center-to-center).
• Built-In Accessory Hook: Front 8 mm hook holds calipers, nozzle wire brushes, or hanging gauges.
• High-Tolerance Grip: Precision clearances prevent L-shaped wrenches and tall tools from tipping over during high-speed printing vibrations.
• Open Source: Full parametric OpenSCAD source files and printable models available on GitHub under Cold Front Forge.

HARDWARE & COMPATIBILITY

This mod requires the official Creality K2 SE / K1 SE transparent enclosure:

• 2x M3-0.5 x 12 mm bolts (Phillips or Hex)
• 2x M3 hex nuts
• 2x M3 flat washers (Use these on the inside of the enclosure to distribute pressure and protect the acrylic panel from cracking)

MODEL VARIANTS & FILES

Files are provided as individual STLs and a pre-configured 3MF project profile:

• K2 SE Tool Kit Holder (k2-se-tool-holder): Full production organizer rack.
• Test Calibration Plate (k2-se-test-plate): Small two-hole test piece to verify your screw spacing and hardware fit before committing to the full print.

RECOMMENDED PRINT SETTINGS

• Orientation: Backplate flat on the build plate.
• Material: PETG or PLA (PETG recommended for enclosure heat resistance).
• Layer Height: 0.20 mm.
• Wall Generator: Arachne (mandatory to hit the 0.2 mm tolerances on small Allen key slots).
• Wall Loops: 4 perimeters for structural rigidity around bolt holes.
• Infill: 25% Gyroid.
• Supports: Tree (Auto) on the build plate only (supports will generate under the lower cups and front hook).
• Brim: None needed on clean textured or smooth PEI sheets.

# SnapNote — 3x3 Sticky Note Stencil System & Desk Caddy

SnapNote turns standard 3" x 3" sticky notes into crisp checklists, bulleted notes, and ruled lists without freehand wobble. Each stencil uses a 2-sided downward alignment lip along the top and left edges so it registers squarely on the pad while leaving the bottom and right edges open for easy peeling, plus 45-degree chamfered channels that guide 0.5 mm and 0.7 mm gel pen tips smoothly without snagging.

The companion desk caddy keeps everything together in one compact footprint: a front pad tray with a thumb scoop for single-sheet peeling, a 5-slot tiered and 12-degree angled stencil rack that keeps every pull-tab label readable, dual 13 mm vertical pen wells, and four underside pockets for 8 mm adhesive silicone feet. Files are open-source and customizable via OpenSCAD under the Cold Front Forge project.

EDITIONS & FILES

• Desk Caddy (snapnote-caddy.stl): All-in-one 3x3 pad tray, 5-slot angled stencil rack, and dual pen wells.
• Base Blank (snapnote-base.stl): Blank 3x3 registration stencil for custom layouts.
• Checklist Stencil (snapnote-check.stl): 6 ruled lines with 4.0 mm filleted square checkboxes.
• Bullet List Stencil (snapnote-bullet.stl): 6 ruled lines with 3.6 mm circular bullet dots.
• Ruled Lines Stencil (snapnote-lines.stl): 7 evenly spaced edge-to-edge ruled lines.

RECOMMENDED PRINT SETTINGS

• Orientation: Flat on the build plate (no supports needed; stencils print face-down).
• Material: PLA or PLA+.
• Layer Height: 0.20 mm.
• Walls: 3 to 4 perimeters.
• Infill: 15% to 20% Gyroid.

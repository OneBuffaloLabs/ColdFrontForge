# Cold Snap Exhibit — Architectural Pedestal Plinth & A-Frame Easel

Give your grails the museum treatment they deserve. The Cold Snap Exhibit is a classical architectural pedestal plinth and A-frame easel designed to showcase PSA graded slabs, Cold Snap Slabs, and standard 35pt toploaders on your desk or display shelf.

Features:
- Dual-Fit Resting Shelf: Features a forward 79.0mm x 2.6mm channel for standard 35pt toploaders and a stepped rear 85.0mm x 8.8mm pocket for PSA graded slabs and magnetic One-Touch cases.
- Support-Free Printing: Every stepped moulding, capital flare, and bevel adheres to strict 45-degree overhang limits, printing 100% support-free flat on the build plate.
- Tapered A-Frame Easel Spine: Tilts cards at an optimal 78-degree viewing angle with an open central truss window to reduce weight and keep center of gravity firmly over the 92x80mm base footprint.
- Modular Museum Plaque: Includes an optional notched-corner label plate that fits flush into the front column recess for custom labeling or two-tone filament contrast.
- Non-Slip Feet: Four 8.5mm circular pockets on the base accommodate adhesive rubber bumpers.

Print Settings:
- Orientation: Print base flat on the build plate (Z=0).
- Supports: None required.
- Infill: 15-20% Gyroid.
- Walls: 3-4 perimeters.
- Layer Height: 0.16-0.20 mm.
